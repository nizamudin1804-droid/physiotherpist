Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first physiotherapy visit.

No referral is legally required! Under Direct Access healthcare laws, you can book and consult directly with our licensed physiotherapists immediately. If your private insurer requires a GP referral slip for reimbursement, our administrative desk will guide you through the 1-page paperwork.
Your senior clinician conducts a full medical history review, orthopedic joint & muscle tests, functional movement screens, and nerve tension evaluations. We immediately explain the diagnosis in plain English, begin gentle hands-on treatment on Day 1, and map out your custom recovery timeframe.
Please wear comfortable, loose-fitting athletic clothes that allow easy exposure of the affected area (e.g., shorts for knee/hip injuries, sleeveless top for shoulder/neck concerns). We also provide clean clinic gowns and private changing rooms if you are coming straight from work.
Most acute muscle and joint issues resolve within 4 to 6 sessions over 3–5 weeks when combined with our home exercise protocol. Severe post-operative cases or chronic degenerative conditions may take 8 to 12 weeks of phased rehabilitation. We provide honest prognosis metrics at your first visit.
