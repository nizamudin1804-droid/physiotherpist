Our Story & Clinical Vision

Setting the Standard for Modern, Evidence-Based Physiotherapy

Founded in 2011 by orthopedic and sports clinicians, KineticPulse was built on one fundamental principle: patients deserve dedicated, unhurried, one-on-one attention from board-certified clinicians using modern, non-invasive technology.

We don't leave you hooked to passive machines in curtained rooms. Every single session is an active partnership between you and your specialized physical therapist, integrating manual therapy, progressive neuromuscular loading, and advanced modalities.
