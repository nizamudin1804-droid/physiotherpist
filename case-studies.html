We track objective clinical metrics: pain scores (VAS), degrees of joint motion (goniometry), limb strength symmetry (dynamometry), and time to sports return. Explore 4 in-depth clinical case audits below.

Clinical Case #KP-104

L5-S1 Extruded Herniated Disc with Radiculopathy in a 44-Year-Old Runner

Surgery Avoided
Initial Clinical Presentation

Patient presented with severe right lumbar pain radiating past the knee into the lateral foot. MRI confirmed a 7mm L5-S1 posterolateral disc extrusion compressing the S1 nerve root. Spine surgeon recommended discectomy. Patient sought conservative care first.

Initial Pain Score:8.5 / 10
Straight Leg Raise (SLR):Positive at 35°
Achilles Reflex:Diminished (1+)
Walking Tolerance:< 50 yards
Targeted Physiotherapy Protocol
  • •Weeks 1–3: Mechanical axial decompression traction + McKenzie prone extensions to centralize nuclear disc material.
  • •Weeks 4–7: Sciatic neurodynamic flossing to relieve perineural edema + ultrasound-guided multifidus motor control.
  • •Weeks 8–12: Deadlift hinging retraining, hip abductor loading, and progressive return-to-run treadmill intervals.
Discharge Pain Score:0.0 / 10
Straight Leg Raise (SLR):88° (Negative)
Functional Outcome:Completed 10K Race at Week 14
Clinical Case #KP-219

Post-Op ACL Reconstruction (Patellar Tendon Autograft) in 20-Year-Old Footballer

Return to Play Cleared
Initial Clinical Presentation

Collegiate midfielder suffered non-contact pivot rupture of right ACL and medial meniscus tear. Underwent arthroscopic BTB autograft reconstruction. Began physiotherapy on post-op Day 4 with severe quadriceps arthrogenic inhibition.

Initial Knee Extension:-8° (Extension lag)
Quadriceps Index:42% of uninjured limb
Effusion (Swelling):Grade 2+ Trace
Weight Bearing:Two crutches
Phased Physical Therapy Program
  • •Months 1–2: High-frequency NMES for VMO recruitment, patellar mobilization, restoring 0° hyperextension.
  • •Months 3–5: Heavy eccentric leg presses, Nordic hamstring curls, and single-leg balance perturbations.
  • •Months 6–7.5: Dual force-plate triple hop testing, reactive cutting, and full contact scrimmage clearance.
Limb Symmetry Index (LSI):98.4% (>90% standard)
Knee Flexion Range:142° (Full match to left)
Clearance:Full 90-Min Match Play at 30 Wks
Clinical Case #KP-312

Adhesive Capsulitis (Frozen Shoulder) in 58-Year-Old Executive

Full Range Restored
Initial Clinical Presentation

Patient had severe progressive restriction in left shoulder for 8 months following minor slip. Night pain woke him 4–6 times per night. Could not fasten seatbelt or put on a winter jacket.

Shoulder Abduction:65° (Normal 180°)
External Rotation:12° (Normal 90°)
Sleep Score:0 uninterrupted nights
Shockwave & Capsular Mobilization
  • •4 Radial Shockwave Sessions: Targeted at the posterior & inferior glenohumeral capsule.
  • •Maitland Grade IV Glides: Specific inferior distraction to decompress subacromial space.
  • •End-Range Home Protocol: Overhead pulley stretches and active assisted external rotation.
Final Abduction:176° (Normal mobility)
Final External Rotation:85°
Outcome:Pain-free golf swing restored
